# Continuous training during lifecycle maintenance and Qwen inference

Lifecycle collection shares the claim/publication fence (740139) with workers.
Keep that fence and the owned-writer lock: removing either would allow stale
reachability to delete newly published evidence. Inventory disk usage and pending
file names before taking the fence. Extract arbitrary-depth node artifact
references with PostgreSQL JSONPath, validate uncatalogued paths, and register only
missing references in a batch. Actual filesystem operations still validate paths.
Archive at most four old graphs per sweep; archive paired facts and exact recipe
identities in the retirement transaction, then compact immutable memory in a
separate transaction after releasing the claim and writer locks. Record actual
fence duration in maintenance evidence.

The two-candidate primary queue can empty during a genuine 4B call. The steward
may then launch one independent two-node CPU continuation campaign. Its frozen
baseline is the primary selected recipe on the exact primary dataset/protocol;
its child is an untried registered feedback-neighbor/global recipe. Exclude every
recipe in the actual Qwen logical request, including both selected and unselected
options. Recheck the live calling attempt, lease, draw window, novelty and queue
before admission. Only one continuation may be outstanding.

The compute worker handles the continuation's short planning jobs in its compute
lane. Primary compute and formal work sort ahead of continuation work. Existing
eight-process, one-thread fits share the aggregate eight-CPU training slice;
memory/disk/write, checkpoint, lease and draw gates remain unchanged. This adds
no inference and changes no Qwen profile, cadence, token budget or acceptance.
When the actual call ends, finish the current bounded pair and admit no new pair.

Continuation results retain prior-only origin provenance, parameter hashes,
paired100/50/10 outcomes, negative results and durable recipe exclusions. They
are independent development trials with no owning report program: they cannot
move the primary retained parent or become official recommendations. Cached
baseline origins and freshly executed child fits are counted separately. The
in-flight request fixture used in isolated scheduling acceptance is explicitly
not a newly performed Qwen call or forward predictive evidence.
