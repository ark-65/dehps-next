# Separately fitted red and blue output specialists

The retained joint model supplies two separate products:100 red combinations and
one blueTop1. Joint research formerly required every blue experiment to replace
the red output at the same time. This coupled selection rule can discard useful
one-colour development results for an unrelated other-colour regression.

## Observed bottleneck

Campaign5318 completed seven children with no improvement; all seven had red
and blue nested-window vetoes. An audit of120 recent children found no joint
promotions, no blue log loss better than uniform, and no red Brier score better
than uniform. Four children nevertheless improved blue100 while retaining blue
50/10:11/8/2,13/6/2,11/6/2 and12/7/2 against10/6/2. Their red windows failed.
This is evidence for testing output composition, not evidence of a future edge.

Three fresh diagnostic fits also found strongly opposed shared-encoder red/blue
gradients (cosine about-0.75 to-0.76). Neural blue training cross entropy was about
0.879, versus2.877/2.905/2.737 on32 prior-held-out labels; uniform is2.773.
Gradient opposition alone does not establish harmful transfer, and these neural
head diagnostics are not the final ridge-adjusted forecast. They motivate
ablation and expose an overfitting concern; they do not prove its resolution.

## Numerical contract

`retained-red-blue-specialist-v1` freezes the entire red recipe, including
ridge residual correction, as the retained ridge48/64/40/history512 procedure.
Each `split_blue_{raw,fft,ridge,newton}_v1` recipe supplies the blue procedure's
parameters. Each origin fits both base models afresh on its legal prior prefix,
then retains only the red probabilities from the fixed component and blue
probabilities from the variable component. This separates output selection;
the base neural fitters' internal losses have not been changed.

The composed model preserves the canonical marginal-log-odds red ranking and
single blueTop1. Publication uses the same fresh procedure as validation.
Compound anchors retain both components, optimizer and RNG state, mathematical
state, version and digests. Restore checks component identities and actual
states. Both components must have real parameter updates. Receipts count both
components' gradient steps and component fits, separately from origin counts.
All fitting remains in the shared eight-CPU training slice.

The new fitter has a new numerical identity. Old recipe memory, scores, models,
predictions and reports are not rewritten or silently remapped. The retained
base model is re-evaluated under the new release before deployment is accepted.
Base-fitter compatibility is separately checked against frozen old source on
actual prior prefixes. Formal qualification and paired100/50/10 promotion gates
remain unchanged; no pruned child may be relabeled accepted.

## Frozen structural comparison

Freeze the four discovered component recipes before execution. Use real Worker
jobs and Next PostgreSQL for the retained baseline and four composed candidates
on current100 and two predeclared, disjoint earlier100 blocks. Check every
current-origin blue probability against its discovered source and every red
probability against the fresh retained baseline, plus current publication and
anchor restore. This is new fitting of a structural experiment; repeating the
current discovery window does not provide independent confirmation. Earlier
windows remain historical audits, not protected or forward evidence.

Activate only after the complete recorded comparison matches dataset, new
numerical contract and fixed red procedure, preserves red outputs bitwise and
has a current original-gate pass. Record older-window failures as well as gains.
Activation occurs after the old frozen campaign completes and preserves its
accepted parent until an actual new child passes the original gates.

The new topic retires the coupled regularization/capacity/representation
rotation. Initial alternatives include the four frozen blue specialists and
four base-procedure controls. Subsequent bounded proposals vary the blue
component; none can implicitly replace the fixed red procedure. Every completed
batch still appends actual red/blue windows, paired deltas and promotion results.
This is a justified structural research direction, not a claim that continuous
hyperparameter search or repeated labels can establish generalization.

The first owned campaign explicitly reproduces the reviewed best current
composition through ordinary fenced evaluation and the original promotion gate.

untried compositions. First-child evaluation carries the same parent, anchor,
